What we can't reach

Stated plainly — we only find overspend where there's a per-use bill to check.

Flat subscriptions
Nothing to leak against — value flips to right-sizing plans and seats.
Self-hosted models
No token bill at all — it becomes a compute-utilization question.
Chat (ChatGPT / Claude.ai)
No data path today — research only, not promised.
Why it's still defensible
We audit on providers, not for them; the pricing surface gets more complex every release, so the audit's value grows with it.

Risks we're watching

⚑ A provider builds its own audit feature
Customers won't trust a provider to check its own bill — like a bank auditing its own fees. We audit on providers, not for them.
⚑ Observability tools move into recovery
They're partners, not rivals — their data feeds us. Recovery needs pricing history, claims, and enforcement: a different product.
⚑ Model prices keep falling — does waste shrink?
Prices fell ~80% and spend still doubled. Each new token type adds a billing axis — the leak surface grows with the meter.
⚑ Is recovery alone a business?
No — it's the wedge. It wins the customer, then becomes monitoring (recurring) and spend control (the durable revenue).
⚑ Standard recovery is small (0.5–2%)
It's the entry "health check." The large dollars are in enterprise SLA recovery, priced on what we recover.
⚑ Chat usage has no data path
Kept as research, not promised. BYOK is the credible route; seat right-sizing the fallback.
⚑ Enforcement means sitting in the request path
Opt-in, one-line setup, ~20–80ms, only after trust is built by observing first.
